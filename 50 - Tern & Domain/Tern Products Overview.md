# Tern Products Overview

*Parent: [[Tern Systems]]*

---

## Active Products

### [[Polaris]]
- Full ATM system: ACC, APP, TWR
- Replaces [[TAS]]
- Used in Budapest (mentioned as test case in docs)
- Has Electronic Flight Strips + Air Situation Display
- Handles [[Inter-sector Coordination]] and [[Inter-centre Coordination]]

### [[ISDS]] — Integrated Situation Display System
- Air situation display
- Developed + maintained by Tern Customer Service
- Deployed at **Isavia** (Iceland)
- Correlates surveillance tracks → flight plans
- Clearances delivered via [[FDPS]], not ISDS (yet — on Isavia roadmap)

---

## End-of-Life Products

### [[TAMS]] — Tern ATS Message Switch
- Routes/switches ATS messages (AFTN/CIDIN/AMHS)
- Operational at Gufunes, Iceland
- EOL: not selling to new customers
- Handles [[OLDI]] and other ATC message protocols

### [[TAS]] — Tern ATC System
- Predecessor to [[Polaris]]
- Combined ASD + Electronic Flight Strips
- Tower, APP, ACC capable
